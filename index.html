The Cup
Starts Gameweek 25: 5 groups of 4, each team playing the other three in its group home and away (GW25–GW30). The winner of each group goes straight through to the Quarter-Finals, along with the single best runner-up across all five groups (ranked by points, then goal difference, then goals scored). The other four runners-up enter a Play-Off (GW31–GW32, two legs, away goals separate a tie) for the two remaining Quarter-Final spots. The Quarter-Finals (GW33–GW34), Semi-Finals (GW35–GW36) and Final (GW37–GW38) are all two-legged ties, away goals apply throughout. Groups are drawn closer to the time. Winner: £650 · Runner-up: £350.
